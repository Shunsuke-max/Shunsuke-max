## Selected projects

### [FoldLens — Product & Engineering Portfolio](https://github.com/Shunsuke-max/foldlens-portfolio)

A local-first research workspace for interpreting AlphaFold 3 predictions, originally built for OpenAI Build Week 2026 and developed into a broader scientific software project.

FoldLens integrates molecular visualization, confidence metrics, deterministic cross-model analysis, and a tool-using AI research agent. The current development also includes a macOS application and metered AI service infrastructure, with scientific calibration and configured web-service validation ongoing.

**Technologies:** TypeScript, React, Express, OpenAI API, Electron, Supabase, Stripe

[Original public implementation](https://github.com/Shunsuke-max/foldlens) · [macOS releases](https://github.com/Shunsuke-max/foldlens-releases/releases)

### [Part5 Pro — Product & Engineering Portfolio](https://github.com/Shunsuke-max/part5-pro-portfolio)

An iOS learning app for TOEIC Part 5, available on the App Store. It connects diagnostic assessment, grammar and vocabulary practice, learning records, and spaced review. The portfolio documents learning design, a development-version audit of 3,966 questions, and ongoing UX and accessibility work.

**Technologies:** Swift, SwiftUI, Core Data, Combine, GitHub Actions, Fastlane

[App Store](https://apps.apple.com/jp/app/part5-pro/id6748593708)

### [Protein Design — Computational Research Portfolio](https://github.com/Shunsuke-max/protein-design-portfolio)

Computational protein design research undertaken for the Anthropic × Adaptyv Protein Design Competition 2026. The portfolio documents literature review, scientific problem formulation, computational research workflows, reproducibility, and the distinction between predicted structures and experimental evidence.

**Tools:** Python, Jupyter Notebook, Google Colab, biomolecular design and structure prediction models

### Hitokoto English（ひとこと英語）

An iPhone app for practicing what to say in everyday situations. Learners put their thoughts into English, then use AI feedback to check whether their wording conveys the intended meaning and sounds natural.

### [EIKEN Grade 1 Speaking](https://github.com/Shunsuke-max/eiken-grade1-speaking)

A lightweight learning website with 300 reusable English expressions and 900 example sentences. It supports search, category filters, randomized practice, and Japanese translations, without external dependencies.
